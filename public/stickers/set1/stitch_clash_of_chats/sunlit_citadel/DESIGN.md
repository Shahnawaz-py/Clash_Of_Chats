## Brand & Style

This design system channels the triumphant, tactile spirit of fantasy strategy fortress-builders under bright daylight. The visual language blends classic tabletop materials—sun-baked stone, polished golden studs, carved warm timber, and aged parchment—with contemporary interface precision.

- **Design Movement:** Tactile Skeuomorphism modernized through structured vector geometry, physical extrusion shadows, and clean digital ergonomics.
- **Atmosphere:** Exuberant, victorious, dependable, and rich with craft. Surfaces feel physical, chiseled, and substantial rather than flat or ethereal.
- **Key Motifs:** Heavy extruded bottoms on interactive surfaces ("squishy" physical press depths), gold rivet fasteners, carved timber containers, parchment paper cards, and vibrant gemstone status beacons.

The palette reproduces the sun-drenched masonry, timber architecture, and precious metal stockpiles of a fortified citadel.

## Typography

The typography unites bold, physical impact with crisp readability. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Sora` provides wide, robust geometric proportions reminiscent of carved crests, banners, and stone reliefs. Titles use tight letter tracking and high weights (700–800) to project structural stability.
- **Body & Data:** `Be Vietnam Pro` balances warm humanist curves with high x-height and open counters, ensuring optimal legibility across parchment layers even under bright sunlight styling.
- **Numbers & Counter Badges:** Rendered in `Sora` with uppercase tabular numerals for resource tickers (gold, elixir, gem tallies).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a modular fortress hierarchy with content encased in physical frames, banner ribbons, and compartmentalized strongboxes.

- **Grid:** 12-column adaptive grid on desktop (max container `1280px`), condensing to a 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column column structure on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px baseline sub-grid with strict 8px progression for container interior padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`).
- **Bevel & Border Space Allocation:** Outer boundaries of cards reserve 3px to 4px internally for faux-carved structural borders without shrinking textual content zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through physical extrusion and multi-layer bevels rather than floating blur effects.

- **The Extruded Bevel Technique:** Interactive elements sit atop a darker bottom ledge (e.g., `4px` solid border on bottom edge, `2px` on sides and top). When active or pressed, the element translates down by `3px`, reducing the bottom shadow to `1px` to simulate a physical mechanical press.
- **Parchment Surface Tiers:**
  - **Recessed Well:** Inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.15)`) over `#EBDCCB` for inventory slots, progress track troughs, and text inputs.
  - **Neutral Slab:** Flat `#F4ECE1` with a `2px` border in `#6B4024` for basic cards.
  - **Elevated Plank / Modal:** Raised timber frame with outer cast drop shadow (`0 8px 0 #3A1E0E, 0 12px 16px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.25)`).
- **Rivet Studs:** Corner anchors feature `8px` circular accents styled with a light upper gradient, dark underside crescent, and center dot to evoke brass construction bolts.

## Shapes

Shapes emulate hand-hewn planks and rounded stone masonry:

- **Base Radius:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) on common cards, tooltips, and badges, giving elements a thick, chipped-stone corner profile.
- **Pill Geometry:** Tabs, primary stat capsules, and combat action triggers use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to create ergonomic, punchy tactile controls.
- **Framing Insets:** Rectangular containers feature chamfered corner notches or circular rivet studs placed at coordinate intersections.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Golden Vault Button:** Background gradient `#FFD54F` to `#F5B823`. Top highlight `inset 0 2px 0 #FFF2A8`. Heavy base border `4px solid #B28212`. Text is bold `#2C1810` with a subtle white drop shadow underneath (`0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Battle Crimson Action Button:** Background gradient `#EF5350` to `#D50000`. Base border `4px solid #8E0000`. Text is white with text-shadow (`0 1px 2px #3A1E0E`).
- **Emerald Upgrade Button:** Background gradient `#7CB342` to `#5EA832`. Base border `4px solid #33691E`.
- **Interaction:** `:hover` brightens the surface gradient; `:active` translates `translateY(3px)` with base border reduced to `1px`.

### Wooden Plank Headers
- Section dividers take the form of horizontal timber beams (`#6B4024` background with subtle wood grain lines, double horizontal groove borders `#895333` top, `#3A1E0E` bottom).
- Header titles sit atop timber beams enclosed in arched parchment medallions or golden ribbon banners.

### Cards & Strongbox Containers
- **Container Structure:** Dual-wall system. Outer wall is a `3px` solid timber border (`#6B4024`). Inner surface is warm parchment (`#F4ECE1`).
- **Rivets:** Positioned `6px` from all four card corners using dual-tone brass dots (`#F5B823` with `#895333` base).

### Tabs & Pill Selectors
- Housed inside an aged-sandstone recessed track (`#EBDCCB`, rounded full).
- Inactive tabs feature muted sepia text (`#7C5A48`). Active tabs snap forward as physical raised golden or white-vellum pills with a `2px` drop bottom edge.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Set inside recessed trenches with `#EBDCCB` background and `#4A2B1D` borders (`2px`).
- Inset top shadow simulates carved parchment cavities.
- Active focus displays a vibrant `#F5B823` outer ring (`3px`) with `#2C1810` text.

### Progress Meters & Resource Bars
- Troughs use an extruded stone trench with dark inner shadow.
- Fill bars use rounded caps with diagonal gloss highlights in Emerald Green (Elixir/XP) or Amber Gold (Coins), flanked by circular gold medallion counters on the leading edge.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped tokens with high-contrast borders (`1.5px solid #6B4024`), uppercase typography in `label-sm`, and glossy glass jewel accents.
